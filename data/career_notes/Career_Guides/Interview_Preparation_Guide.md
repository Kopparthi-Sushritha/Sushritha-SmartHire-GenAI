# Interview Preparation Guide

## 1. Purpose

This guide helps a fresher prepare for technical, project, behavioral, and role-specific interviews.

## 11. Project Questions

Expect:

- Why did you build it?
- What problem does it solve?
- What was your contribution?
- Why did you choose the technology?
- What was difficult?
- How did you test it?
- What would you improve?
- How would you scale it?
- What did you learn?

Know every important line of code in a project you claim.

## 18. Mock Interview Process

Repeat:

1. Pick a topic.
2. Answer without notes.
3. Record the answer.
4. Identify gaps.
5. Review the concept.
6. Answer again.

## 20. Final Preparation

Before an interview:

- Read the job description.
- Identify required technologies.
- Review your projects.
- Practice likely questions.
- Prepare concise explanations.
- Test your microphone/camera if needed.
- Keep your resume available.

The goal is not memorizing answers. The goal is understanding concepts and communicating your reasoning clearly.
